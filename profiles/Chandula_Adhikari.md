Chandula Adhikari is a Research Assistant at the Multidisciplinary AI Research Centre (MARC), University of Peradeniya, leading two of the centre's research groups: the **Computational Biology & Bioinformatics** pillar and **Generative AI for Dermatology**. Chandula holds a **BSc Eng (Hons) in Computer Engineering, First Class**, from the University of Peradeniya, and before joining MARC was a Temporary Instructor in the Department of Computer Engineering.

Chandula's research lies at the intersection of computational biology and deep learning, with a focus on single-cell genomics, gene regulation and generative models for biomedical data, in particular models built around the underlying biology whose assumptions remain interpretable.
